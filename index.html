ECON4850: Class Experiment #2

Introduction

Welcome to the ECON4850 experiment participation website. Please read these instructions carefully before participating.

Throughout this experiment, we will ask you a series of questions. Depending on your answer to these questions, you may earn different amounts of money. After the deadline for submitting Experiment 2, one student will be selected at random from everyone who completes the experiment and submits their data file on time. Then one of that student's questions will be selected at random, and that student will be paid, in cash, based on the option they chose in that question. Any question could be the one that counts. The maximum possible payment is $40, so please pay attention throughout the experiment.

The experiment will take approximately one hour of your time and consists of several short sections, which you will complete one after the other. We encourage you to be in a quiet area without distractions while participating.

Please use a laptop or desktop computer (not a phone or tablet), and complete the whole experiment in one sitting. If you leave or reload the experiment page partway through, your answers will be lost and you will have to start again.

When you finish, a data file will automatically attempt to save itself to your computer. Please keep this file somewhere convenient: you will need to upload it to Courseworks.

Once you are ready, please click the button below to start the experiment.
